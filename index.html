About Me

I am Iako Latsabidze, a Junior Frontend Developer with a strong interest in creating modern, responsive, and user-friendly web experiences. I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, focusing on clean structure, responsive layouts, and interfaces that are both visually appealing and easy to use. I enjoy turning ideas and design concepts into functional websites and improving small details that make the overall user experience better.

I am currently building my professional experience through practical projects and continuous learning. I spend time improving my JavaScript knowledge, DOM manipulation, responsive design techniques, and the way different interface components work together. I believe that good frontend development is not only about writing code, but also about understanding design, usability, and how users interact with a website.

I am motivated, detail-oriented, and always open to learning new technologies and better development practices. My goal is to continue growing as a Frontend Developer, gain real-world experience, work on more challenging projects, and gradually build strong expertise in modern web development.
